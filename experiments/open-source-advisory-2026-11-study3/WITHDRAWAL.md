# Study 3: withdrawn before any search (19 October 2026)

This study is withdrawn by the operator before block 1 was selected. No search ran, no pull request was selected or read, and no result exists. The preregistration is unchanged and stays as signed; this note is the only addition.

## Why

Study 3 asked how often the advisory reader's `differs` notes on public agent-written pull requests are genuine discrepancies, counted by pull request and by claim kind.

On 19 October 2026 a separate base-rate study answered the question underneath it. It sampled 174 public coding-agent pull requests from September 2026 and checked 514 claims of the kinds Dunstan reads against GitHub's records. Of the 302 claims those records could settle, 1 was contradicted. Most claims about tests and test counts could not be settled from the records at all.

With genuine discrepancies that rare, a 350-pull-request sample would estimate the advisory reader's false-accusation rate but say almost nothing about genuine catches. Study 2 had already found its genuine false claims concentrated in 4 pull requests. The operator has stopped work on Dunstan as a detector of false completion claims, so the study's result would not change a decision.

## What stays

Studies 1 and 2, their records and labels, stay published as they are. The claim format and the checker remain available under their licences.
